关于我们

Who We Are

Red Dragons — 红龙少林太极和功夫学院 — is a school rooted in the living tradition of Shaolin martial arts.

We trace our lineage directly to the Shaolin Temple, the birthplace of Chan Buddhism and the cradle of Chinese martial arts. Every technique we teach has survived centuries because it works — because the body obeys the same laws of physics it always has.

Our instructors hold deep respect for the original teachings and pass them on with integrity, discipline, and joy. Whether you are stepping onto the mat for the first time or refining a lifetime of practice, you are welcome here.

传统

Strictly Shaolin

The Shaolin Temple (少林寺), nestled in the Song Mountains of Henan Province, China, has been the heart of Chinese martial arts for over 1,500 years. Its monks developed a system of movement that unified physical health, spiritual cultivation, and self-defence into a single, coherent discipline.

At Red Dragons, we do not mix or modernise the tradition. We teach it as it was handed down — form by form, principle by principle — because those who came before us already did the physics.

少林一脉，传承不息。
The Shaolin lineage flows on, never ceasing.
